    FAQ

    Common questions

    How are my keys protected?

    Keys are coded internally and are not labelled with your address. Access is only for agreed services and every visit is documented.

    What does a home check include?

    A visual check of doors, windows, visible leaks, water, electricity, humidity/smell, balcony or terrace if accessible, and a photo report after the visit.

    Do you manage tourist rentals?

    Not at this stage. We focus on property care, key holding, scheduled home checks, arrival preparation and coordination services.

    Can you open for a technician or delivery?

    Yes, when authorised by the owner. Waiting time and urgent visits are charged separately.

    Can Arrival Box be done without keys?

    No. Groceries are placed inside only when Luma has authorised access through key holding, key collection, concierge/neighbour or another approved arrangement.
