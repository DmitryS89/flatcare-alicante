    FAQ

    Common questions

    Do you sell groceries?

    No. We provide a shopping and apartment preparation service. Products are bought fresh by request and charged at the actual supermarket receipt price.

    Do you manage tourist rentals?

    Not at this stage. We focus on property care, key holding, home checks, pre-arrival preparation and coordination services.

    Can you buy baby formula?

    Only if you provide the exact brand, age/stage and any allergy instructions. We do not choose baby formula ourselves.

    What happens if something is unavailable?

    You can choose: replace with a similar item, contact you via WhatsApp or skip the item.

    Do you provide legal or tax services?

    No. If legal, tax or tourism-registration help is needed, we can coordinate with independent professionals.
